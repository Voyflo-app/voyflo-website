Go Pro

Questions, answered.

Are the prices real-time?

They're AI estimates, shown as per-person ranges so you can budget honestly. Live pricing and direct booking links are on the roadmap — the plan tells you what to book; today you book it where you like.

How fast is "about a minute"?

Flights, hotels, and your budget typically appear within seconds. The full day-by-day itinerary streams in right after — longer trips take a little longer, and you can browse while it finishes.

Can I change a plan after it's generated?

Yes. Compare the three flight and hotel options on every plan, edit the trip details — dates, route, pace — and regenerate as part of your monthly allowance.

Does multi-city really work?

Up to six legs in one trip. Each leg gets its own flight options, each city its own stays, and the itinerary and budget run across the whole journey.

Which phones is Voyflo on?

Both — Voyflo launches on Google Play and the App Store together. The app is built mobile-first, because that's where trips get planned.

What about my data?

Your account is protected with verified email sign-up and secure Google sign-in. Your trip details are used to build your plans — not sold. Full details in our privacy policy.
